How to use vshn.app

Loading data

Open CSV expects headers Date, Description, Category, Amount, Type, Method. Use yyyy-mm-dd dates, Debit or Credit types, and UPI, Cash, or Card methods.

Editing

Edit CSV unlocks the table. Click a row, use Add Row or Delete Row, then Save Edits. Invalid cells turn red; click Errors: N to jump to the first one.

Exporting

Export CSV downloads the table once all errors are fixed.

Reading the chart

Choose Debit, Credit, Saving amount, or Saving rate. Filter by Category or Method, switch Daywise or Cumulative, and choose Day, Week, Month, or Year. Drag to pan, hover a bar for its exact value, and double-click to reset zoom.

Stat cards

Total saving, credit, and debit follow the current filter. Point value shows the hovered or latest point.
